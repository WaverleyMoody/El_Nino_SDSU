The latest daily sea surface temperature data starting from January 1, 2026, are animated in this video for you to visually monitor the 2026/27 super El Niño. The growth of the Niño 3.4 regional average temperature accelerated in April 2026. The growth continues throughout May, June, and July. It is expected that this growth will continue until the end of 2026. 

Instead of using sea surface temperature anomalies to indicate El Niño, as done by most scientists, we show El Niño using the full values of sea surface temperature. El Niño is an expansion of warm surface water in the tropical Pacific. You can see that in April 2026, the water warmer than 28 degrees Celsius in the west was already connected to that in Ecuador's coastal region. A warm west-to-east belt across the tropical Pacific is formed and continues to evolve.
